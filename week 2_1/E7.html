Stir-fry Chicken

What You Need

  • 300g Chicken slices
  • Soy Sauce
  • 100g Beansprouts
  • Pepper
  • Oyster Sauce

Steps

  1. Pour some oil into a heated pan
  2. Add the chicken slices
  3. Once chicken slices are cooked, add the Beansprouts
  4. Add Soy sauce, oyster sauce and pepper
  5. Turn off the heat and serve
